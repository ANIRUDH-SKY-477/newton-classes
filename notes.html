🔬 Science Smart Notes

Physics, Chemistry aur Biology ke quick revision points.

Chemistry

Balanced equation mein dono taraf har element ke atoms ki sankhya barabar hoti hai.

pH 7 se kam acidic, 7 neutral (25°C par), aur 7 se zyada basic hota hai.

Corrosion se bachav: painting, oiling, galvanising ya alloying.

Biology

Major life processes: nutrition, respiration, transportation aur excretion.

Photosynthesis mein light aur chlorophyll ki presence mein carbon dioxide aur water se glucose banta hai, oxygen release hoti hai.

Genes DNA ke segments hote hain jo inherited traits mein role play karte hain.

Physics

Ohm’s law: V=IR.

Electric power: P=VI=I²R=V²/R.

Mirror/lens numericals mein sign convention aur units ka dhyan rakhein.
